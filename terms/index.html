OsmoSwitcher reads the keys you type on your computer to fix words typed in the wrong layout, as described in the Privacy Policy. Don't use it to watch other people's typing. The app is provided as is: we work to make it fix words correctly, but it can make mistakes, and double Shift or switching the layout back undoes a fix.
